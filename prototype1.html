Prototype 1

The first physical iteration of the ESP32 C3 Zero enclosure — a proof-of-concept focused on basic dimensions and fit.

Overview

Key features

Prototype 1 was designed using basic CAD measurements taken directly from the ESP32 C3 Zero datasheet. The goal was to produce a working enclosure as quickly as possible to test fundamental fit and dimensions.

  • Simple rectangular two-part shell (base + lid)
  • Friction-fit lid closure — no fasteners or clips
  • No ventilation holes or cutouts
  • No port or button access openings
  • Printed in PLA at 0.2 mm layer height

Evaluation

Pros & cons

Advantages

  • Fast and simple to design and print
  • Confirmed overall board dimensions were correct
  • Provided a physical baseline to improve from
  • Low material cost for testing

Problems & limitations

  • Board fit was too loose — shifted inside the case
  • No ventilation caused noticeable heat build-up
  • USB-C port completely blocked by case wall
  • Boot and reset buttons inaccessible
  • Lid was very difficult to remove once fitted
Planned improvements for Prototype 2:
Tighten board tolerances so the module sits firmly without movement. Add ventilation slots on the side walls. Cut openings for the USB-C port and tactile buttons. Replace the friction-fit lid with a snap-fit clip mechanism for easier access.
