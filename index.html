About this project

Why a custom case?

The ESP32 C3 Zero is a compact Wi-Fi and Bluetooth microcontroller used in IoT projects. Without a suitable enclosure, the board is vulnerable to physical damage, poor ventilation, and difficult mounting. This project explores three iterations of a protective case designed and 3D-printed to solve these problems.

Side-by-side comparison

Design comparison

Criteria Prototype 1 Prototype 2 Final Design
Fit and size Slightly loose; board shifted inside Improved tolerances; snug fit Precise fit; board fully secured
Ventilation / cooling No ventilation cutouts; heat build-up Side vents added; better airflow Optimised vent pattern; efficient cooling
Ease of printing Simple geometry; printed quickly More complex; minor support issues Refined for minimal supports; clean print
Lid / closure Friction fit; difficult to remove Snap-fit attempted; not fully reliable Secure snap-fit; easy open/close
Button / port access No cutouts for ports or buttons USB-C hole added; boot button exposed All ports and buttons fully accessible
Overall quality Functional proof of concept Clear improvement; some issues remain Ready for real-world deployment
